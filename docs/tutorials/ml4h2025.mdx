# MEDS ML4H 2025 Tutorial

_Building a model with MEDS: from the key design principles through data extraction, task definition, and model
building._

The MEDS tutorial at ML4H 2025 walked through building a model with MEDS on the open-access MIMIC-IV demo, in
interactive notebooks. Its materials are in the
[ML4H 2025 tutorial repository](https://github.com/Medical-Event-Data-Standard/MEDS_ML4H_2025_Tutorial).

These tutorial pages were first featured there, and render its notebooks:

1. [Getting Familiar with MEDS](./MEDS_intro.mdx)
2. [Converting to MEDS](./converting_to_MEDS.mdx)
3. [Extracting a Prediction Task](./extracting_prediction_tasks.mdx)
4. [Build a Tabular Baseline](./build_a_model_tabular.mdx)
5. [Build a Neural Network](./build_a_model_NN.mdx)

Since the event, the [tutorials](./index.mdx) have gained a [pre-processing step](./preprocessing_MEDS_data.mdx),
a step to [compare with MEDS-DEV](./compare_with_meds_dev.mdx) and a page on
[visualizing and exploring MEDS data](./visualizing_MEDS_data.mdx).
